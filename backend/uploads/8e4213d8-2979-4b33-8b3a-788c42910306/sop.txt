Standard Operating Procedure - Heat Treatment Line

1. Raw material (steel blanks) is received at the receiving dock and staged for processing.

2. Blanks are loaded into FCE-03, the primary batch furnace, for the heat treatment cycle of
   the steel components. Furnace chamber temperature must be maintained per the active work
   order specification for the full soak time.

3. After heat treatment, parts move via the cooling belt to reach ambient temperature before
   further handling.

4. Parts are inspected at the inspection station for dimensional conformity. Rejected parts
   are routed to scrap; conforming parts are logged and staged for the next step.
