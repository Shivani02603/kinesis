Raw steel billets and coil stock are received and staged at the yard. Stock is charged into FCE-3, the continuous furnace, for austenitizing heat treatment. Treated stock proceeds to the cooling conveyor for controlled ambient cooling. Cooled stock is inspected for Rockwell hardness and surface defects before packaging. Conforming stock is logged and staged for the packaging line, where a protective coating and strapping are applied before palletizing. Rejected stock is routed to the temper-and-retest area. The line's active objective is maintaining hardness within the active work order specification.
