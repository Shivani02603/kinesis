Syrup concentrate and treated water are received and staged at the batching area. Ingredients are blended in MX-1, the batch mixer, to produce finished beverage base. Finished beverage is piped to FL-4, the rotary filler, dispensing product into bottles. Filled bottles proceed to the capping machine for sealing. Sealed bottles are inspected for fill level and seal integrity before labeling. Conforming bottles are logged and staged for the labeler before palletizing. Rejected bottles are routed to the drain-and-scrap line. The line's active objective is maintaining fill level within the active work order specification.
