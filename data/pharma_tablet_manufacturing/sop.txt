Raw active ingredient and excipients are received and staged before processing. Material moves to GRN-1 for wet granulation, forming uniform granules. Granules are then compressed into tablets on TP-2, the primary rotary tablet press. Compressed tablets proceed to the coating pan for a protective film coat. Coated tablets are inspected for dimensional and weight conformity before packaging. Conforming tablets are logged and staged for blister packaging on the packaging line. Non-conforming tablets are routed to reject handling for destruction record-keeping. The line's active objective is maintaining tablet hardness and weight within the active work order specification.
