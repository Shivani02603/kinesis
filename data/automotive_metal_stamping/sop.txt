Steel coil stock is received and staged at the uncoiling station. Coil stock is fed into SP-7, the progressive stamping press, forming raw metal parts. Stamped parts proceed to the robotic weld cell for spot-welding of sub-assemblies. Welded assemblies are inspected for weld penetration before painting. Conforming assemblies move to the paint booth for electrostatic coating. Painted assemblies are logged and staged for final assembly. Defective welds are routed to the rework/scrap area. The line's active objective is maintaining weld quality within the active work order specification.
