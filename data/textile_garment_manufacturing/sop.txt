Raw yarn bales are received at the staging dock before entering production. Yarn is fed to WL-2, the weaving loom, producing greige fabric rolls. Fabric rolls proceed to the dyeing vat for color application and fixation. Dyed fabric is inspected for weave and color defects before cutting. Conforming fabric moves to the cutting table for pattern cutting. Cut panels are logged and staged for the stitching line to assemble finished garments. Defective fabric rolls are routed to the seconds/scrap bin. The line's active objective is maintaining fabric quality within the active work order specification.
