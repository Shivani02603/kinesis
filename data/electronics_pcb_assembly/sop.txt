Bare PCB substrate panels are received and staged with solder paste applied by stencil. Panels move to SMT-1 for automated placement of surface-mount components. Placed panels proceed through RO-4, the reflow oven, to solder components in place. Reflowed boards are inspected at the AOI station for solder joint defects. Conforming boards proceed to wave soldering for through-hole components. Completed boards are logged and staged for final test and packaging. Defective boards are routed to the rework station. The line's active objective is maintaining solder joint quality within the active work order specification.
